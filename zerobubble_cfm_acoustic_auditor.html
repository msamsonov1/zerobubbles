Governing Equation 1 Radiation Force Field

Gor'kov Acoustic Radiation Potential on Hydrogen Bubbles

In microgravity, buoyancy forces vanish ($g \to 0$). Entrained vapor bubbles ($H_2 \text{ gas}$) are manipulated solely through the gradient of the Gor'kov acoustic potential $U$, directing bubbles toward pressure antinodes or nodes depending on the acoustic contrast factor $f_1, f_2$:

$$U = 2\pi R_b^3 \rho_f \left[ \frac{\langle p^2 \rangle}{3 \rho_f^2 c_f^2} f_1 - \frac{\langle v^2 \rangle}{2} f_2 \right]$$
$$\mathbf{F}_{\text{rad}} = -\nabla U, \quad f_1 = 1 - \frac{\rho_f c_f^2}{\rho_b c_b^2}, \quad f_2 = \frac{2(\rho_b - \rho_f)}{2\rho_b + \rho_f}$$

Because liquid hydrogen density $\rho_f \approx 70.85\text{ kg/m}^3$ exceeds hydrogen vapor density $\rho_b \approx 1.34\text{ kg/m}^3$ by over fifty-fold, $f_1 \ll -1$, driving micro-bubbles with massive primary acoustic radiation force $\mathbf{F}_{\text{rad}}$ directly toward the acoustic pressure nodes.

Primary Force: $\sim 10^{-4}\text{ N per bubble}$ $LH_2$ Speed of Sound $c_f \approx 1100\text{ m/s}$
Governing Equation 2 State Inversion

Effective Medium Acoustic Velocity & Wavelength Shift

When vapor voids populate the duct, Wood’s effective medium relation and Foldy’s acoustic dispersion law alter the bulk compressibility $K_{\text{eff}}$, causing an instantaneous measurable shift in the acoustic standing wave wavelength $\lambda$ for a constant drive frequency $\omega$:

$$\frac{1}{c_{\text{eff}}^2} = \left( \alpha \rho_b + (1-\alpha)\rho_f \right) \left[ \frac{\alpha}{\rho_b c_b^2} + \frac{1-\alpha}{\rho_f c_f^2} \right]$$
$$\lambda(P, \alpha) = \frac{2\pi c_{\text{eff}}(P, \alpha)}{\omega} = \lambda_0(P) \cdot \left[ 1 - \frac{4\pi c_0^2 n_b R_b}{\omega_0^2 - \omega^2 + 2i\delta \omega} \right]^{-1/2}$$

By continuously measuring standing-wave wavelength $\lambda$ and duct static pressure $P$, the real-time software computes the deviation $\Delta \lambda = \lambda_{\text{meas}} - \lambda_{\text{pure}}(P)$. Any divergence detects void fractions down to $\alpha = 0.001\%$ long before bulk thermal sensors react.

Void Sensitivity: $\Delta \alpha \approx 10^{-5}$ Sampling Bandwidth: $2.5\text{ kHz}$
Governing Equation 3 Impeller Destruction

Rayleigh-Plesset Vapor Collapse & Net Positive Suction Head

If an entrained bubble enters the turbopump inducer, the local pressure drop plunges below saturation pressure $P_{\text{sat}}(T)$. As the bubble enters the high-pressure impeller stage, violent asymmetric Rayleigh-Plesset collapse generates microjets ($v_{\text{jet}} > 1000\text{ m/s}$), eroding titanium-aluminide blades within seconds:

$$R \ddot{R} + \frac{3}{2}\dot{R}^2 = \frac{1}{\rho_f} \left[ P_{\text{bubble}}(R) - P_{\text{duct}}(t) - \frac{2\sigma}{R} - \frac{4\mu_f \dot{R}}{R} \right]$$
$$\text{NPSH}_{\text{margin}} = \frac{P_{\text{inlet}} - P_{\text{sat}}(T_{LH_2})}{\rho_f g_0} - \frac{v_{\text{inlet}}^2}{2 g_0} \ge \Delta H_{\text{crit}}$$

ZeroBubble maintains $\text{NPSH}_{\text{margin}} > 0.35\text{ bar}$ equivalent by preventing thermodynamic vapor expansion and micro-bubble seeding before fluid crosses the inducer inlet plane.

Governing Equation 4 Closed-Loop Actuation

Multiphase PZT Phase Modulation & Acoustic Bubble Sweeping

Upon detecting anomalous $(\lambda, P)$ divergence, the controller shifts the drive signals across the piezoelectric transducer array from a stationary standing wave into an asymmetric traveling wave with spatial phase velocity $v_{\text{phase}} = \Delta \phi / (\Delta x \cdot \omega)$:

$$p(x,t) = \sum_{k=1}^{4} A_k \sin\left(\frac{2\pi}{\lambda_k} x - \omega t + \phi_k(t)\right), \quad \phi_k(t) = \phi_0 + k \cdot \Omega_{\text{sweep}} t$$
$$\langle \mathbf{F}_{\text{net-drift}} \rangle = \frac{4\pi R_b^3}{3} \nabla \langle E_{\text{kinetic}} - E_{\text{potential}} \rangle + \frac{2\pi}{\lambda} \cdot \Pi_{\text{acoustic}}$$

This continuously accelerates acoustic nodes radially inward or axially toward secondary gas vent recirculation ducts, purging 100% of bubbles away from the engine turbopump manifold without mechanical moving parts.
